Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Short version: CTRLS stores everything on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal or financial data. Period.

1. Introduction

CTRLS ("we," "our," or "the app") is a native iOS application designed to help you track investments, transactions, goals, and recurring bills. This policy explains what data we collect — and why the answer is essentially none.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CTRLS does not collect, store on remote servers, transmit, sell, rent, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials of any kind
  • Financial account numbers, brokerage logins, or bank credentials
  • Transaction history, holdings, or portfolio data
  • Goals, recurring bills, or budget information
  • Receipt photos or any image you save in the app
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics tokens
  • Location data, contacts, or calendar information

3. Where Your Data Lives

All financial records, portfolio holdings, transactions, goals, bills, and receipt photos you create in CTRLS are stored locally on your device using the app's private sandboxed storage. The data never leaves your iPhone unless you explicitly export it yourself (for example, via CSV export or a system share sheet).

Receipt images captured by the in-app camera or photo picker are saved into the app's private photo library and are not accessible to other apps.

4. No Accounts, No Login

CTRLS does not require registration, sign-up, or login. There is no account to create, no password to remember, and no server-side profile to manage. The app is a tool, not a service.

5. No Third-Party Services

CTRLS does not integrate with analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting services, marketing platforms, social media plugins, or any third-party data processor. The app makes no outbound network requests for tracking purposes.

6. No Connection to Brokerages or Exchanges

CTRLS does not connect to any brokerage, bank, exchange, or financial institution. All data is entered manually by you and reflects your own records. We have no access to your actual financial accounts and never will.

7. iOS System Permissions

CTRLS may request the following iOS permissions, only when needed and only to perform features you initiate:

  • Camera: Used solely to capture receipt images that you choose to attach to a transaction. Photos are stored locally in the app's private sandbox.
  • Photo Library (read-only): Used solely to attach an existing photo to a transaction. CTRLS does not modify your photo library.
  • Haptics: Light tactile feedback during interaction; no permission prompt and no data involved.

You can revoke camera or photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Data Backup

Standard iOS device backups (encrypted iCloud backups or iTunes/Finder backups) are controlled by you through your Apple ID and system settings. CTRLS does not control or access the contents of those backups. If you delete the app, its locally stored data is removed with it unless restored from a backup you have previously made.

9. Children's Privacy

CTRLS is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no data at all, this app is safe for all ages from a privacy standpoint. If you are a parent and have questions, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this document is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Material changes will be communicated through an in-app notice where feasible. Because CTRLS does not collect contact information, we cannot email you about changes.

11. International Users

Because CTRLS does not transfer any data off your device, the cross-border data transfer regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPL) are largely not applicable to data processed by the app. You retain full control of any data you choose to export yourself.

12. Your Rights

Because all data is stored on your device, you can exercise complete control at any time by editing or deleting records within the app, exporting your data, or uninstalling the app. There is no remote profile to delete because none exists.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at Mefepzavap@icloud.com.
