Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your financial data — portfolios, transactions, goals, and bills — lives exclusively in CTRLS's local storage on your iPhone. We never upload it to any server.

Do I need to create an account?

No. CTRLS has no login, no registration, and no account. Just open the app and start tracking.

Can CTRLS connect to my brokerage or bank?

No. CTRLS is a fully manual tracker by design. You enter your holdings and transactions yourself. This is intentional — it means we never have access to your financial credentials, and your data stays on your device.

How do I back up my data?

Your data is included in standard iOS device backups (encrypted iCloud backups or iTunes/Finder backups). You can also export data as CSV directly from the app's Reports section.

How do I delete my data?

Open the app and remove records individually, or uninstall the app to erase all locally stored data. Because no data lives on a server, deletion is instant and complete.

What iOS version is required?

CTRLS requires iOS 16 or later on iPhone.

Does CTRLS support iPad?

The app is optimized for iPhone. On iPad it runs in compatibility mode. A native iPad layout may be added in a future update.

Can I import data from another finance app?

Yes — use the CSV import path available in the Reports section. You can map standard columns to CTRLS fields during import.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email Mefepzavap@icloud.com with your iOS version, iPhone model, and a description of the issue. Screenshots are very helpful.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled by the App Store. Use the "Report a Problem" link in your App Store purchase history to request a refund from Apple.

What to Include in Your Email

To help us resolve your question quickly, please include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • CTRLS version number (found in the app's Settings screen)
  • A clear description of the issue or question
  • Screenshots or screen recordings if applicable
